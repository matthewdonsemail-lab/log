# Using ListeningKit

You do not need to be technical for Reddit. X and Facebook need one extra step (the [helper](/docs/guide/helpers)).

## 1. Sign in

Open **https://tremendous-seahorse-330.convex.site** and choose **Continue with Google**. There is no username or password to make. Your matches and logins belong to your account only.

## 2. Pick where to listen

On the first screen choose the platforms your customers use: Reddit, X, Facebook. You can change this later.

## 3. Install the Chrome extension

The extension does one job: it reads that you are logged in to a site and hands the app a token, so the app can read that site as you.

1. Download **listeningkit-extension.zip** from the onboarding screen, or open `/listeningkit-extension.zip` on the site.
2. Unzip it.
3. In Chrome, Edge or Brave open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, and choose the unzipped folder.
4. Pin the ListeningKit icon so it is easy to find.

## 4. Tell us about your business

Paste your website address when asked. ListeningKit reads the page and fills in your business name, what you offer and how you sound, so matches can be judged against your business. If reading is not switched on for the site, it keeps just the name it can tell from the address and you carry on. Use a public website address; addresses like `localhost` are refused. You can read a site once every 30 seconds.

## 5. Connect a platform

1. In the same browser, open the site (for example reddit.com, x.com or facebook.com) and **log in**.
2. Click the ListeningKit icon. It should say *Ready. You are logged in to* that site.
3. Click **Copy token**.
4. In ListeningKit, paste it into the token box on the connect step (or **Settings → Connections**) and click **Connect**.

<Callout type="warning">
The token is your login. Treat it like a password. ListeningKit stores it encrypted, never shows it again, and never sends it to your browser. Use **Settings → Connections → Disconnect** to remove it.
</Callout>

For X and Facebook, **use an account you can afford to lose.** These sites can restrict accounts that are automated (see the helper page).

## 6. Add a phrase

The **Free plan** allows **one phrase per platform** and **one connected account per platform** at a time. When a platform is full, the Keywords page tells you so and the button is off; remove the phrase you have to add another. **Settings, Billing** shows what you are using. Pro, with more, is planned but not available, so there is nothing to buy.

Open **Keywords**, choose a platform, type the phrase, and click add.

- **Reddit:** also choose the community, for example `smallbusiness`. The server checks it about every 10 minutes. Press **Check now** to check straight away.
- **X and Facebook:** just the phrase. Matches appear once the [helper](/docs/guide/helpers) is running.

Tips for good phrases: use what a customer would say, not your product name. Short, specific phrases such as `need a bookkeeper` work better than single words.

Each phrase row shows when it was last checked and how. "By your helper" means your own computer read it; "from a backup source, posts may be hours old" means Reddit's feed was unavailable and a slower mirror was used.

## 7. Read your matches

Matches appear under the phrase list, newest first. Each card shows:

- the post text, the author and a link that opens the original post;
- a **score badge** such as `90 · Ready to buy`, with the reason in one line (needs an OpenAI key on the server; otherwise no badge);
- likes and comments where the platform provides them.

Choose **Best matches first** to sort by score instead of time (the default is **Newest first**).

## 8. Get strong matches by email

Open **Settings → Notifications**, enter your email address, choose how strong a match must be (60, 70, 80 or 90 and up), tick **Send me these emails**, and press **Save**. Press **Send a test email** to check it arrives (it can take a minute; check spam). You get one short email with your best new matches, at most 5 in an email and at most one email every 10 minutes. Each match is emailed once. Only matches that have been scored can be emailed, so this needs scoring to be on. Untick the box to stop.

## Troubleshooting

| You see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| "not checked yet" (Reddit) | The first check has not run. | Press **Check now**. |
| "waiting for the helper on your computer" | An X or Facebook phrase has not been read yet. | Run the [helper](/docs/guide/helpers). |
| "from a backup source, posts may be hours old" | Reddit blocked the fast feed. | Fine to use, or ask the owner to set up Reddit's API keys. |
| The extension says you are not logged in | You are logged out in that browser. | Log in to the site, click the icon again. |
| "X refused the saved login" (helper) | The login expired or X logged the account out. | Log in again, copy a fresh token, reconnect. |
