# Tokens

A token is the extension's proof that **you** are the one browsing that platform. It's a compact export of your session for a single site.

## Where to copy it

<Tabs items={["Any platform"]}>
<Tab value="Any platform">

1. Log into the platform in Chrome.
2. Open the ListeningKit extension.
3. Copy the token it shows and paste it on the [connect screen](http://localhost:3000/onboarding).

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## What it grants

- Read-only access to the public signals you browse as that account.
- No posting, no DMs, no account settings — ever.

<Callout type="warning">
Treat tokens like passwords. Anyone with a token can read signals as that account. The demo keeps tokens in your browser's local storage only.
</Callout>
