# ListeningKit

Live social listening. Pick the platforms you care about — we capture what real customers are saying and cluster what they keep repeating.

<Callout type="info">
This is the hackathon build. Reddit, X and Facebook are read live today; start with the [Guide](/docs/guide) for what works, how to use it, and how to develop on it. Other pages in this site describe an earlier mock design.
</Callout>

## How it works

1. **Pick your platforms.** Facebook, X, or Reddit — whatever your customers talk on.
2. **Install the Chrome extension.** It quietly captures the public signals from the pages you browse.
3. **Paste your tokens.** The extension exports a per-site token; one paste per platform.
4. **Watch it cluster.** Similar signals group into topics with volume, tone, and trends.
