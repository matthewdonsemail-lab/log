# log

log runs your social accounts from the terminal or from your AI agent. It works with X, Reddit, Facebook and Discord, and can sign in to LinkedIn so far. You type `log …` commands yourself, or connect `logMcp` and let your agent call the same commands as tools.

## Get started

1. [Install log](install.md): one line on macOS, Linux or Windows.
2. [Add your accounts](accounts.md): sign each one in and check it works.
3. [Connect your agent](mcp.md): Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or any MCP client.

Then read [Using log well](usingLog.md).

## Use cases

[Use cases](useCases/README.md) are the jobs log is built for, step by step: replying to people on a topic, reading your DM inbox, listening to Facebook groups and more. Start here when you know what you want done.
